## Delete Your Online Guest Profile and Data

You can request deletion of your Fap Kolo online guest profile and associated data.

### From the app

1. Leave any active online room.
2. Open **Privacy**.
3. Select **Delete online profile and data**.
4. Confirm your request while connected to the internet.

The app confirms completion after the game service confirms deletion.

### Without access to the app

Email **[Logic.fred1@gmail.com](mailto:Logic.fred1@gmail.com)** with the subject **Fap Kolo — Delete my online profile**.

Include your player nickname and any non-sensitive details that may help identify your profile, such as an approximate date of online play. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a request.

We may ask for additional information to locate the correct profile and verify the request. A nickname alone may not uniquely identify a profile. **Never send your secret guest credential, passwords, or payment card details.**

Parents or guardians may contact us about a child’s profile.

### What is deleted

Deletion removes your online guest profile and all stored multiplayer rooms containing that profile from the live game database. This includes associated room and match information and ends those rooms for other participants.

### What remains

* Local adventure progress, earned rewards, settings, and purchased cosmetics remain on your device.
* Deletion does not cancel purchases, issue refunds, or erase Apple’s transaction records.
* Other players’ local records or screenshots are not removed.
* Playing online again creates a new guest profile.

Online profiles and room records do not currently expire automatically. Uninstalling the app does not delete them.

## In-App Purchases

On iOS, optional cosmetic purchases are processed by Apple.

To restore an eligible purchase, open **Rewards & upgrades → Restore Purchases**, using the Apple Account that originally purchased it.

For a missing or unavailable item, include its name when contacting support. Do not send payment card information.

## Troubleshooting

Please include:

* Device model
* Android or iOS version
* App version and build number
* A description of the issue
* Steps to reproduce it
* A screenshot, if helpful

Remove personal or sensitive information from screenshots before sending them.
